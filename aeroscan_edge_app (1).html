Problem & approach

Problem. Aircraft surface inspection is still largely manual — technicians scanning fuselage panels visually, for hours, at height. Fine cracks, early corrosion pitting, and hairline defects get missed, and any cloud-based CV system depends on connectivity hangars often don't reliably have.

Approach. AeroScan Edge runs a YOLOv8n model, trained on defect imagery and remapped to an aerospace defect taxonomy, entirely on-device — no cloud round-trip. Detections are severity-tagged against EASA-style categories and compiled automatically into a maintenance report.
